# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 16 September 2026**

> **The short version, for young spotters**
>
> - We ask how old you are, and keep only your age group, never your birthday.
> - If you're under 13, you pick a nickname from ones we make up, and we never ask for your email.
> - Your score is private unless you're 13 or over and choose to show it.
> - Under-13s can't post comments, and nobody sees your name in public.
> - To keep your account for good, or to get a prize, a grown-up has to say yes first.
> - We never sell your information, and there are no adverts.
> - Grown-ups can see, download or delete what we hold at any time: [fishspotter.app/parent](/parent).

## How we use your data

- To authenticate you and maintain your account.
- To calculate your points, streak, and leaderboard rank, and to show you your own rank when your score is private.
- To aggregate quiz answers in anonymised form so the community can see identification trends per clip.
- To send transactional emails (account verification, password reset, and messages to parents about their child's account or prize) via our email processor, Resend.
- To post prizes to winners who ask for one.
- To support ecological research into UK marine biodiversity by PEBL CIC as part of our community-benefit mission. Any research outputs use aggregated, anonymised data only.

### Automated processing of community identifications

We use automated processing to aggregate community identifications. When three or more users independently submit the same identification for a clip that has no reference answer, our system records a community consensus and may retroactively award points to those users. This affects only your score within the game and has no legal or similarly significant effect on you, so it is not solely-automated decision-making of the kind restricted by UK GDPR Article 22.

<h2 id="children">Children</h2>

FishSpotter is used by people of all ages, including children at home and at school. We ask everyone their age group before they play, and what we collect and show depends on the answer.

| | Under 13 | 13 to 17 | 18 and over | Not told us yet |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Name they play under | A nickname they pick from names we generate | Their chosen name | Their chosen name | Their chosen name |
| Their own email address | Never collected | Optional | Optional | Optional |
| Public leaderboard and profile | Never | Off unless they switch it on | On unless they switch it off | Hidden until they answer |
| Posting comments | No | Yes | Yes | Not until they answer |
| Optional emails (weekly digest, new clips) | No | Only if they opt in | Only if they opt in | No |
| "Streak about to end" reminders | No | No | Only if they opt in | No |
| Usage analytics | No | With consent | With consent | No |
| ID-guide chat (AI) | No | No | Yes | No |
| Keeping the account on more than one device | A parent or carer's consent | Their own email | Their own email | Their own email |
| Receiving a prize | A parent or carer's consent | A parent or carer's consent | Yes | Not until they answer |

We ask for an age group, not a date of birth, and the answer cannot be changed in the app. If a wrong option was chosen, email us.

### Children under 13

A child under 13 can play without giving us their real name or any way to contact them. We keep only their nickname (which they choose from our generated list, so it cannot be their real name), the species they identify and when, their points, and a cookie that keeps them signed in on their device. We use these only to run the game for them. We do not ask for their name, email address, location, photos or contact details, and nothing about them is shown publicly.

To keep a child's account beyond the device they are using, or to post them a prize, we need their parent or carer's consent:

1. The child gives us a parent or carer's email address. We use it only to ask for consent.
2. We email the parent a notice explaining what we keep, why, and who can see it, with a link to agree or say no.
3. If the parent agrees, we email them again the next day to confirm, with a link to change their mind.
4. If the parent says no, or does not answer within 14 days, we delete their email address and the request.

A parent who has agreed can use the [parent page](/parent) at any time to:

- see the information we hold about their child and download a copy;
- sign their child in on another device;
- stop us collecting any more information, by withdrawing consent;
- delete their child's account and everything in it.

We never disclose a child's information to anyone other than the service providers listed below, who handle it only on our behalf, and we never make it public.

### Children aged 13 to 17

Teenagers can create their own account with their own email address. Their name is kept off the public leaderboard, their profile page and comments unless they choose otherwise, and they get no "streak about to end" reminders. We ask them to check with a parent or carer before signing up. We will not post a prize to anyone under 18 until a parent or carer has agreed by email, and we then arrange delivery with the parent.

### If you think a child gave us information without permission

Tell us at [hello@pebl-cic.co.uk](mailto:hello@pebl-cic.co.uk) and we will delete it.

### How long we keep children's information

We keep children's information only as long as we need it for the reasons above:

- **A parent's email address on a request that is not answered:** deleted when the request expires after 14 days.
- **A parent's email address on a request they decline:** deleted straight away.
- **An under-13's account (nickname, identifications, points):** kept while it is used, and deleted after 12 months with no identifications, or as soon as a parent asks or withdraws consent.
- **A parent's email address on a consent they gave:** kept for as long as the child's account (for an account consent), or until 90 days after the prize is posted (for a prize consent), so that we can show consent was given and handle any delivery problem.
- **A prize winner's postal address:** used only to post the prize and deleted within 90 days of posting. For a child, we get it from the parent, never the child.
- **A school email address on an account that had not told us its age:** we emailed the account once to explain these rules, and remove the address on the date given in that email (14 days later). The account's progress stays.

These deletions run automatically every day, apart from postal addresses, which are handled by email and deleted by our staff.

| Name | Type | Duration | Purpose | Consent |

| **NextAuth session** | Strictly necessary | Until sign-out or after a period of inactivity | A signed, HTTP-only token that keeps you authenticated. | **`fishspotter:sid`** | Analytics (functional) | Until you close the tab (sessionStorage) | A random per-tab id used only to count anonymous usage (see below). It is not a cookie and does not follow you between visits | Set only with your consent (Accept) |

The **fs.anon_seed** cookie is a functional cookie, not strictly necessary, so it is covered by the consent banner shown on your first visit; we only set it where consent permits. You can withdraw your consent for functional cookies at any time by clearing your cookies or by using the cookie controls on the site.

### Anonymous usage measurement

With your consent (the **Accept** option on the banner), we record a small amount of **first-party, anonymous** usage data so we can show our funder, the National Lottery Climate Action Fund, that the project is engaging people. We measure only: when a viewing session starts, which clips are viewed, and how long clips are actively watched. We deliberately **do not** store your IP address, device or browser details, your location, or any identifier that follows you between visits. We do not use third-party analytics, advertising networks, or social-media tracking pixels. If you choose **Essential only**, none of this is recorded. Nothing is recorded for a signed-in account that is under 13 or has not told us its age, whatever the banner says, because a child under 13 cannot give this consent. You can withdraw consent at any time by clearing your cookies/site storage.

## How long we keep your data

- **Account data** (email, display name, hashed password, age group): retained while your account is active and for **24 months after your last sign-in**, after which we prompt you or delete it. Children's information follows the shorter rules in [Children](#children).
- **Quiz answers linked to your account**: retained for the same period as your account data.
- **Comments you post**: retained for the same period as your account data, and deleted with your account. Comments are **public** to other spotters who have identified the same clip. If your leaderboard visibility is switched on and you are 13 or over, they are shown with your display name; otherwise (the default for spotters aged 13 to 17, and always for anyone who has not told us their age) other spotters see an anonymised handle instead, though PEBL staff can still identify you internally to moderate the service. Records of moderation decisions (for example that a comment was hidden, and why) may be kept for a reasonable period after deletion where we need them to meet our legal obligations.
- **Anonymised aggregates** derived from quiz answers: retained indefinitely for ecological research purposes; they cannot be used to identify you.
- **IP addresses**: held in memory only, never persisted, cleared on server restart.
- **Server access logs**: retained by Vercel, our hosting sub-processor, per Vercel's log-retention period (see "What data we collect and why").
- **Survey footage** (the underwater video clips): scientific data collected by PEBL CIC in the field and retained indefinitely. Clips do not contain personal data.

Separately, if you make an erasure request, we will respond and delete your personal data within 30 days. (This is our response time, not a retention period.)

We keep a written security programme for children's information, review it at least once a year, and choose service providers who commit in writing to protecting the data they hold for us.

## Your rights under UK GDPR

You have the right to:

- **Access**, request a copy of the data we hold about you (available at [/api/account/export](/api/account/export) when signed in, or by emailing us). Parents can do this for a child on the [parent page](/parent).
- **Rectification**, correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
- **Erasure**, ask us to delete your account and associated personal data (Art. 17). You can do this yourself from your account settings, or email [hello@pebl-cic.co.uk](mailto:hello@pebl-cic.co.uk) with the subject "Erasure request".
- **Portability**, receive your quiz answers in a machine-readable format.
- **Object**, object to processing we carry out under legitimate interests (for example, the research use of quiz answers, or abuse-prevention use of your IP address and access logs). If you object, we will stop that processing unless we can show compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, rights, and freedoms, or that the processing is needed to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.
- **Restrict processing**, ask us to pause processing while a dispute is resolved.

Where processing relies on consent (the **fs.anon_seed** functional cookie, usage analytics, and a parent's consent for a child), it can be withdrawn at any time through the site's cookie controls, by clearing your cookies, or for a parent on the [parent page](/parent); this does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

To exercise any of these rights, email [hello@pebl-cic.co.uk](mailto:hello@pebl-cic.co.uk). We will respond within one calendar month.
