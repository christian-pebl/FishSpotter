The food web of a seaweed & shellfish farm

All 72 species in the FishSpotter catalogue, joined to what they eat and what eats them. Toggle the farm on and off to see how much of this web the seaweed and shellfish actually build, and click any species to trace its links.

·

The farm gives a bare seabed four things it lacks: hard structure (an artificial reef of ropes, floats and anchors), a kelp-canopy nursery, a rain of detritus and mussel biodeposits that enriches the seabed, and a standing crop of mussels as food. Together they let reef fish, wrasse, blennies, urchins, snails and shellfish-eaters colonise a patch of open sand.

Strip the farm away and the site is bare soft sediment and open water. The structure, kelp and mussels are gone, and with them 25 reef, weed and shellfish specialists that only the farm supports (ghosted); 7 more thin out (faded). What remains is the natural sand-and-open-water community, plus clean-sand animals like the sea potato (marked +) that the biodeposits had been suppressing. The gap between the two views is the biodiversity the farm creates.
